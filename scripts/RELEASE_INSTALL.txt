FlareSimAE v1.0.5

1. Close After Effects.
2. Copy FlareLensAE.aex (Windows) or FlareSimAE.plugin (macOS) into
   the After Effects Plug-ins folder.
3. Open After Effects and find FlareSimAE in Effects & Presets.

Remove older copies before installing. Lenses are built in.
macOS: the plug-in is ad-hoc signed (not Apple notarized).
