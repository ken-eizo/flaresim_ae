FlareSimAE v1.0.4 — Installation

1. Quit Adobe After Effects.
2. Windows: copy FlareLensAE.aex to the After Effects Support Files/Plug-ins folder.
   macOS: copy FlareSimAE.plugin to the After Effects Plug-ins folder.
3. Restart After Effects and search for FlareSimAE in Effects & Presets.

Remove any older copy first. No separate lens files are needed.
macOS is ad-hoc signed (not notarized); approve it only if you trust this download.

Details: https://github.com/ken-eizo/flaresim_ae/blob/main/INSTALL.md
