# Install FlareSimAE

1. Close After Effects.
2. Download the ZIP for your operating system from [Releases](https://github.com/ken-eizo/flaresim_ae/releases/latest).
3. Extract it and copy **FlareLensAE.aex** (Windows) or **FlareSimAE.plugin** (macOS) to your After Effects Plug-ins folder.
4. Restart After Effects. Find **FlareSimAE** in Effects & Presets.

Remove older copies before installing. Lens presets are built in.
macOS: this download is ad-hoc signed and not Apple notarized.
